*Historical sea level data were obtained from these sources: Thailand Marine Department (MD), Thailand Department of Mineral Resources (DMR), University of Hawaiʻi Sea Level Center (UHSLC), Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission of UNESCO (IOC), and Deep-ocean Assessment and Reporting of Tsunamis (DART).

**The blue band and the ± value after the current sea level show an uncertainty of ±3×RMSE. Each station’s RMSE and predictions are computed from its available training data. Training records vary in length, completeness, quality, and recency, which may make some predictions unreliable. These experimental predictions must not be used for navigation, hazard response, engineering, or other safety-critical decisions.

***Sea level predictions are produced by station-specific regression models fitted to historical observations. Each model combines the preceding 30 days of hourly lunisolar TGA components and estimated clear-sky GHI, including nonlinear and cross terms, through spline-smoothed lag coefficients.
