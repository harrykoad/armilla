*Acronychal rising and cosmical setting occur when the star, at the specified altitude, respectively rises at sunset and sets at sunrise. Their dates are given in the Gregorian calendar for the central year only. Clicking each date links to the Armilla page, which displays the celestial sphere for the specified observational parameters.

**The Indian lunar mansions (nakṣatras) and their principal stars (yogatārās) are adapted from the Sūryasiddhānta. Nakṣatras marked with a double asterisk (**) represent alternative forms used in various Indian-influenced cultural traditions. Several bright stars not associated with nakṣatras are also included for reference.

***Acronychal rising and cosmical setting dates shown for selected years are approximate and may differ by up to one day for a given year.
