*Acronychal rising and cosmical setting occur when the star and Sun cross their specified altitudes on opposite horizons: respectively, the star rises as the Sun sets and the star sets as the Sun rises. Their dates are given in the Gregorian calendar for the central year only. Clicking each date links to the Armilla page, which displays the celestial sphere for the specified observational parameters.

**The Indian lunar mansions (nakṣatras) and their principal stars (yogatārās) are adapted from the Sūryasiddhānta. Nakṣatras marked with a double asterisk (**) are alternative asterisms used in various Indian-influenced cultural traditions and are listed separately from their main forms. Several bright stars not associated with nakṣatras are also included for reference.

***Acronychal rising and cosmical setting dates shown for selected years are approximate and may differ by up to one day for a given year.
